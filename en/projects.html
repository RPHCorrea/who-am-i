Educational Technology

Infrastructure Management

Context

Responsible for the full management of the technological environments within the educational unit, including computer labs, administrative equipment, and network infrastructure.

Challenges

Heterogeneous environments, constant need for standardization, laboratory management, and optimization of repetitive processes.

Educational Maker Space

Structure and Management

Context

Responsible for the organization, operation, and technical support of the institution's Maker Space, an environment focused on practical project development, prototyping, and innovation culture.

Challenges

Ensuring the safe and efficient operation of technological equipment, guiding students and teachers in the proper use of tools, and structuring the environment to support interdisciplinary projects.

Role

Operation and maintenance of 3D printers, laser cutters, plotters, CNC milling machines, robotic arms, and prototyping tools. Providing technical guidance to students and teachers in the creation of projects and prototypes, promoting the integration of technology, creativity, and hands-on learning.

Impact

Expansion of the educational use of the Maker Space, increased student autonomy in prototyping solutions, and strengthening of the innovation culture within the institution.

Technologies and Equipment

3D Printers • Laser Cutting • Plotter • CNC Milling • Robotic Arm • Maker Tools
